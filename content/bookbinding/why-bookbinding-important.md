# The Importance of Bookbinding
Bookbinding is importance because it help preserve history, stories, art, and amazing historical records. Bookbinding is an art itself because in this day in age where things can be easily digitalized we tend to forget how good it is to have a hobby and learn different things. Yes the processes can be hard to get a hold of, but the skills, [[types-of-bookbinding|methods]], and [[binding-process|process]] can be unreplaceable. 

>Bookbinding has created history. 
## Without Books What Would Happen?
Without the art of bookbinding ai wouldn't have advanced as it did today. Without Bookbinding we wouldn't have historical records or other methods of learning. Many people relay on learning off of a physical copy of a book instead of digital versions. In addition, without the ability or knowledge of bookbinding people wouldn't have the creative freedom of expressing themselves through materials, textures, and cover designs. 

### What Has Bookbinding created?

* Journals
