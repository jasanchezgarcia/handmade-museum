# What Tools do You Need? 
To begin your journey you will need to do research what [[types-of-bookbinding|type of book]] you want to create and how you want to do it. After your done deciding, you will most likely need a bone folder, scissors, a painting brush, boxcutter, sewing needles, waxed thread, ruler, glue, cutting mat, and an awl. The majority of these items are commonly found at your local craft store or other convenient stores. The rest of the items can be bought after deciding if you like hobby because at the end of the day we don't want these items collecting dust. 

>Before you start creating, you will need the necessary tools and materials. 

![[bookbinding-materials.jpg]]
## What Materials do You need? 
According to your creative decisions and [[binding-process|process]] you will have the choice of selecting leather, decorative paper, or book cloth. However, some materials will never change like chipboard, end pages, headbands, mull, paper, and a sewing templets. Mull is a material similar to cheese cloth and some people recommend it as a alternative to mull, but doesn't have the same strength as mull. Headbands can be created or bought, they will usually sit at the end of the text block or paper block. Any additional materials depend on personal preface.
