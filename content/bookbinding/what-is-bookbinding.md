# The History of Bookbinding
To understand what bookbinding is, we will have to start with the history and how it came to be. One of our first examples will be the Egyptian scrolls because they constructed something that will hold their important documents. As time progressed, different needs started to appear like creating specific books for drawing, stories, and documents. 

> Everything changes with time
## What Changed? 
People started to get new ideas on how to create durable books. They first started with different sewing methods then they started to use wood to create a hard cover book. Then they used different leather like goat skin, calf skin, sheepskin, a mix of different animal skins, and what is more popular today, bonded leather. These changes needed [[types-of-bookbinding|different binding methods]], [[bookbinding-tools|tools]], and [[binding-process|process]].
