# Bookbinding Process
Bookbinding is a process that involves assembling [[bookbinding-tools|tools and materials]] to create a book or sketchbook. The process typically starts with cutting, organizing, and folding sheets of paper into section called signatures. After assembling the signatures you will sew them together then apply glue and a small peace of mull to secure the spine. After waiting for the glue to dry you will measure the width and heigh of the paper block to cut out your chipboard an end pages. Finally, the book will be pressed while you glue down your preferred material on to the chipboard which will become the case of the paper block. After everything is ready you simply have to place your paper block on the spine and glue your paper block on to the case. This should be the general processes of assembling a book. 

>Greatness comes with great responsibility.  
## Things to Keep in Mind 
When bookbinding there are several important things to keep in mind to make sure that bind is going to hold it's structure over time. The first thing to keep in mind is that the processes can take hours to several days when it comes to gluing items together. Second, cutting your pieces of paper can be a long and boring preparation because you want your text block to look professional after a long day of repeating the same step. Third, is cutting your chipboard according to the grain direction which you test by bending your chipboard and cutting your cover according to the most flexible side. Finally, after your done making your masterpiece you be able to understand [[what-is-bookbinding|why bookbinding is important]]. 

## Step by Step List
* Cut and Assemble Signatures
* Sewing Signatures
* Apply Glue and Mull
* Measure Chipboard 
* Assemble Case 
* Glue case and Paper Block Together
