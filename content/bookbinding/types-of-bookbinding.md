# How Many Types of Bookbinding are Their?
According to the web they are about six way to bind a book they are known as, saddle stitch, perfect bound, case bound, spiral bound, wire bound, and comb bound. Each bound has common martials, but require different skills and time, like the case bound. In addition, bookbinding is not only for books it also for making sketchbooks and different shapes and sizes of notebooks. 

> Bookbinding is and will always be art. 
## What is The most difficult bind? 
As we know we know bookbinding can be time consuming and require lots of skills or [[bookbinding-tools|tools]] depending on what your doing. For example, the self standing book also known as lectern binding can be difficult to make because of it's presides measurements and tringle base panels. Many people have complained how instructions aren't clear or little to no tutorials thus making it hard to make the lectern book.
